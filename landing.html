Core Features

Five core features that power your UX study journey.

📚
Resource Library

94 curated UX resources — Figma, typography, icons, prototyping, and more. Filter by tag or add your own links to the collection.

Three daily reflection prompts — what you learned, what confused you, what's next. Search past entries and watch your growth.
